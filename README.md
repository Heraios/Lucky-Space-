# Lucky Space

Tools for a hard-SF novel series set in the 161st-century Solar System.

## Penpal Clock (`penpal-clock/`)

Open `penpal-clock/index.html` in a browser. For any moment in AD 16000–16100 it shows:

- the light lag between Kahlun (Mars) and Grafin (The Tube, Pluto–Charon), with round trip, trend and solar-blackout warnings;
- each boy's local time (Mars mean solar time; sun-time at the Tube's Pluto end);
- a message planner (send → receive → reply);
- a Mars season tracker: Mars Year, sol, solar longitude, season, season and month start dates, perihelion and aphelion.

`penpal-clock/ephemeris.js` holds precomputed planet positions. It is generated by `tools/ephemeris/`: JPL DE421 initial conditions for 2000, integrated forward with REBOUND (`run.py`, then `export.py`).

## Story bible (`story-bible/`)

Open `story-bible/index.html`. Characters, places, gods and orders, culture and technology, with links between entries, search, continuity notes and open questions. All content lives in `story-bible/entries.js`; it is updated chapter by chapter (currently through *Grafin I*).
